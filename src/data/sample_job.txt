Machine Learning Engineer, NLP
Company: Example AI Labs
Location: Toronto, Canada (Hybrid)

About the role
We are looking for a Machine Learning Engineer to design, build, and ship
natural language processing systems that reach production. You will own
models end to end, from research and evaluation to deployment, monitoring,
and iteration against real user data.

Responsibilities
- Design, train, and fine-tune transformer-based NLP models (LLMs, BERT-style
  encoders) for tasks such as text classification, named entity extraction,
  question answering, and conversational agents.
- Build and maintain data pipelines for data cleaning, annotation, augmentation,
  and versioned training corpora.
- Evaluate models with appropriate offline metrics and online A/B tests, and
  report results clearly to non-technical stakeholders.
- Deploy, serve, and monitor models in production, including latency, cost, and
  reliability trade-offs.
- Collaborate with backend and platform engineers to integrate models into
  services, and with product teams to shape feature direction.
- Stay current with the research literature and apply it selectively to
  production problems.
- Mentor junior engineers and contribute to code review and technical writing.

Requirements
- 3+ years of professional experience building production machine learning or
  NLP systems.
- Strong Python skills, including hands-on experience with PyTorch or
  TensorFlow, scikit-learn, Hugging Face Transformers, and pandas.
- Solid understanding of transformer architectures, embeddings, attention,
  tokenization, decoding strategies, and evaluation methodology.
- Experience with retrieval, ranking, or dialogue systems is a strong plus.
- Familiarity with MLOps tooling: Docker, Git, CI/CD, experiment tracking, and
  model serving frameworks.
- Excellent written and verbal communication; able to explain trade-offs
  clearly.
- MS or PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or a related field, or
  equivalent practical experience.
